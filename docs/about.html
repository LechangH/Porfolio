About Me

Most of my work is influenced by digital median and online communities, especially the gaming world and animation. I often draw style inspiration from game characters, environments, and visual storytelling. Platforms like YouTube, Discord, and other online forums also expose me to different creative styles and ideas from around the world. The one I focus on the most is different type animes styles.

For my personal work, I often choose simple pencil drawing, mostly just black and white. I enjoy drawing characters and ideas that interest me without making the process too complicated. Drawing is one of the ways for me to relieve stress and focus on somethings.

As I continue as a graphic designer, I'm interested in exploring how traditional drawing and digital design can work together. I'm also interested in how different art styles can make the same subject look and feel different.
